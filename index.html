Talk to the AI

Once you've run an analysis, tell it what to fix in plain language — it keeps the photos in mind and revises the marking report above. Examples: "the belly is more cream than white," "there's a dark patch on the left ear you missed," "make the tail solid ginger, no tip." Each reply updates the Marking Report textarea above — click "Paint Stitch Maps From Report" again to push the change into the grids.

Generate Finished Crochet Cat Photo

Sends only three kinds of images to our AI service: the approved colored stitch maps, four fixed 3D-render views (front/left/right/back), and the gray finished crochet-cat reference photo you upload below. Your customer's real cat photos are never sent here — only what you've already approved through Steps 2–3. Coat colors and markings come only from the stitch maps and 3D render; the gray photo is used only to match crochet texture, yarn look, stuffed shape, and construction (ears, tail, safety eyes, embroidered nose/mouth, whiskers) — its gray color itself is not copied unless your stitch maps are actually gray.
Gray Finished Crochet-Cat Reference
This should be a real photo of a finished (undyed/gray) crocheted cat made from the same base pattern — it teaches the image model what "actually crocheted" looks like: stitch texture, yarn thickness, stuffed shaping, proportions, ears, tail, safety eyes, embroidered nose/mouth, whiskers. It is a construction reference only, never a color source.
